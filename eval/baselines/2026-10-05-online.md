# 2026-10-05 online baseline

## Scope

- Dataset: `eval/cases.jsonl`
- Knowledge base: `产品手册`
- Data plane: 6 Markdown documents, 106 chunks, 106 vectors
- Retrieval: candidate pool `TopK * 20`, rerank to Top 7
- Chat model: `qwen3.7-plus`
- Embedding model: `text-embedding-v4`, dimension 1024
- Rerank model: `qwen3-rerank`

All 13 retrieval-applicable cases hit their expected document. The out-of-scope stock question refused as expected. The composite Mesh-and-price question produced one verified part and one locally refused part. The follow-up Mesh question resolved the pronoun correctly and answered with verified citations.

## Notes

- The run used the production `/api/chat/stream` workflow and the same 14-case expectations as `rag-app eval`.
- One batch attempt hit a transient `provider_unavailable` during validation. A retry of the affected case succeeded, and all final saved results passed.
- Single-question latency was commonly tens of seconds; composite questions took longer because each subquestion runs retrieval, judging, generation, and validation.
